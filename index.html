ABOUT

We’re Iziel and Reed. Kofa builds straightforward websites for small businesses that don’t have one yet. If people currently find you through word of mouth or social media, we give them a clear place to learn what you do and how to reach you.

Iziel designs and builds the site. Reed helps shape the words, details, and business information your customers actually need. We work with you directly, from the first conversation to the day your site goes live.

Our starting point is simple: a website that looks like your business, works on phones, and makes it easy for someone to get in touch.

QUESTIONS

WHAT DO YOU NEED FROM ME?+

We’ll ask about your business, what you offer, and how people should contact you. If you have a logo or photos, bring them. If not, we can talk through what will work for your first site.

DO I NEED A DOMAIN NAME ALREADY?+

No. We can help you choose and connect one. The cost of a domain and any hosting is separate from the website build.

CAN I UPDATE THE SITE LATER?+

Yes. You’ll receive your site files and a short handoff guide. If you’d rather have us make future changes, we can discuss that separately.
