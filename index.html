ABOUT

We’re Iziel and Reed. Kofa is a small, Raleigh-based service for independent shops that want to spend less time on repetitive customer questions and more time on the work in front of them.

Iziel builds the systems and automations. Reed brings retail experience, client conversations, and the details that make a workflow feel natural. Together, we make useful tools for the realities of a busy shop floor.

We start with one practical problem: the emails and DMs you answer again and again. Your customers still hear from you. Your team just has a better starting point.

SERVICES

01

CUSTOMER REPLIES

We turn the questions you get every day—hours, returns, sizing, order updates—into clear, ready-to-personalize replies. Your team can find the right draft, make it yours, and send it without starting from scratch.

02

YOUR VOICE & POLICIES

We learn how your shop actually speaks to customers and how your policies work. Each reply is built around your real answers, so your team stays in control of every conversation.

03

TEAM HANDOFF

We organize everything into a simple library and show your team how to use it. No new platform to learn and no automatic messages going out to customers.
